## 🏆 108 Master Activity List

### 🚗 I. Car Culture, JDM, Racing & Driving
1. **Guided Night Trip to Daikoku PA JDM Car Meet (Yokohama)**: Book a guided van/car night tour to Yokohama’s Daikoku Parking Area (R34 GT-Rs, RWBs, tuning legends).
2. **Fuji Speedway Circuit & Motorsport Museum (Mt. Fuji)**: FIA Grade-1 racetrack, Motorsports Museum, pit lane tours, and supercar circuit driving.
3. **Up Garage Parts Hunt**: Japan’s premier used JDM car parts chain store for rare badges, accessories, and components.
4. **Street Go-Karting through Tokyo**: Drive custom karts through Shibuya and Akihabara in costume *(IDP required)*.
5. **Nissan Crossing (Ginza)**: Nissan's flagship glass showroom featuring hypercar concept models and GT-R heritage builds.
6. **Super Autobacs Tokyo Bay**: Multi-story automotive megastore packed with racing seats, performance parts, and demo cars.
7. **Fun2Drive Hakone Sports Car Rental**: Rent an R34 GT-R, Supra, or AE86 to drive the legendary Touge mountain passes near Mt. Fuji.
8. **Honda Welcome Plaza Aoyama**: Honda HQ exhibit featuring F1 engines, Type R prototypes, and robotics history.
9. **Toyota City Showcase / GAZOO Racing**: Inspect Toyota’s GAZOO Racing lineup, rally cars, and concept vehicles.
10. **Liberty Walk Tokyo / Saitama**: Check out famous widebody supercar builds and custom stance cars up close.
11. **Spoon Sports / Type ONE Workshop**: The ultimate sanctuary for Civic Type-R and Honda performance tuning enthusiasts.
12. **RE Amemiya RX-7 Workshop (Chiba)**: Rotary engine legends and D1GP drift car tuning house.
13. **APEXi & HKS Tuning Shops**: Visit iconic aftermarket tuning brands around Greater Tokyo.
14. **Subaru World HQ Showroom (Ebisu)**: WRX STI rally heritage models and cutaway boxer engine displays.
15. **Bingo Media / BH Auction Showroom**: Ultra-rare Japanese collector cars, NISMO 400Rs, and vintage Skylines.
16. **Midnight Highway Cruise on Wangan (C1 Loop)**: Night taxi or rental cruise along Tokyo’s elevated Shuto Expressway.
17. **D1 Grand Prix / Ebisu Circuit Visit**: Track down Japanese drift culture roots and exhibition runs.
106. **Pro Drift Taxi Ride-Along**: Buckle into the passenger seat of a tuned 500hp Silvia/Chaser next to a pro D1 drift driver sliding sideways.
107. **Live Circuit Race Spectating (Sept 9–15)**: Spectate live racing (**Formula Drift Japan 2 Sept 12–13 / Super GT / WEC 6 Hours of Fuji**) with pit passes.
108. **Fuji Speedway Hotel & Museum**: Luxury Hyatt Unbound trackside hotel with circuit-view balconies, private onsen, and motorsport museum.

### 🤖 II. High-Tech, Robotics, Gaming & Views
18. **Tokyo Skytree Night View (World's 2nd Tallest Structure — 634m)**: Top observation deck at night for a 360° view of Tokyo's neon skyline.
19. **Miraikan — National Museum of Emerging Science & Innovation (Odaiba)**: Robotics museum featuring androids (Otonaroid), AI robotics, and space exploration.
20. **teamLab Borderless (Azabudai Hills)**: Immersive digital art museum where whole rooms are filled with living light and sound.
21. **Shibuya Sky Observation Deck**: 360° open-air rooftop 230m above the Shibuya Scramble crossing.
22. **Akihabara Multi-Floor Arcades (GiGO / Hey)**: Retro cabs, claw machines, and fighting games across 6 floors.
23. **Ghibli Museum (Mitaka)**: Studio Ghibli's animation museum with rooftop Totoro, original cells, and exclusive short films.
24. **Super Nintendo World (Universal Studios Japan, Osaka)**: Real-life Mario Kart ride with AR goggles and wristbands.
25. **Gundam Factory & Statue (Odaiba)**: Giant life-sized transforming Unicorn Gundam statue.
26. **Nintendo Tokyo Flagship Store (Shibuya Parco)**: Flagship store with life-sized statues of Mario, Link, and Pokemon.
27. **Super Potato Akihabara**: Legendary retro video game store and arcade museum (Famicom, Game Boy, arcade classics).
28. **Den Den Town (Nipponbashi, Osaka)**: Osaka's otaku and electronics sanctuary packed with retro hobby shops.
29. **Small Worlds Tokyo**: Miniature indoor theme park featuring 1:80 scale Evangelion hangar and Space Center models.
30. **Joypolis Indoor Arcade Park (Odaiba)**: SEGA-operated indoor amusement park with VR rollercoasters.
31. **Red° Tokyo Tower**: High-tech esports, VR gaming, and motor racing simulator arena inside Tokyo Tower.
32. **Capcom Store & Square Enix Cafe**: Specialized game-themed cafes and official merchandise shops.
33. **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building Observatory**: Free 45th-floor view of the endless Tokyo skyline.

### 🍱 III. Food, Izakayas, Kobe/Hida Beef & Sake
34. **A5 Hida Beef & Kobe Beef Tasting in Takayama Old Town**: Torch-seared A5 Hida beef nigiri over rice crackers in Takayama's Edo streets.
35. **Samurai Edo Post Town Sake Flights (Nakasendo / Narai-juku)**: Stroll 400-year-old samurai post towns and sample regional craft sake flights.
36. **Tsukiji Outer Market Sushi Breakfast**: Fresh nigiri, tamagoyaki skewers, and sea urchin between 6:00 AM–8:00 AM.
37. **Shinjuku Ramen Run (Ichiran / Fuunji / Afuri)**: Rich Tonkotsu or dipping tsukemen ramen with soft-boiled egg and chashu pork.
38. **Omoide Yokocho ("Memory Lane")**: Smoky narrow alleyway in Shinjuku packed with charcoal yakitori stalls.
39. **Dotonbori Street Food (Osaka)**: Fresh hot Takoyaki (octopus balls) and Okonomiyaki (savory cabbage pancake at Kiji).
40. **A5 Kobe Beef Teppanyaki (Kobe Day Trip)**: World-famous A5 wagyu cooked live on a stone grill in front of you (Misono).
41. **Jiggly Soufflé Pancakes (A Happy Pancake)**: Fluffy Japanese pancakes that wobble on arrival.
42. **Golden Gai Bar Hopping**: 200+ tiny 6-seat micro-bars tucked inside 6 narrow alleys in Shinjuku.
43. **Fushimi Sake District Brewery Tasting (Kyoto)**: Walk historic canal-side sake houses and taste Gekkeikan craft varieties.
44. **Traditional Multi-Course Kaiseki Dinner**: 10+ artistic seasonal courses served during a traditional dining experience.
45. **Kuromon Ichiba Market (Osaka)**: Fresh sea urchin, wagyu skewers, and giant king crab legs cooked on site.
46. **Convenience Store Raid (7-Eleven / Lawson / FamilyMart)**: Egg salad sandwiches, Famichiki fried chicken, and onigiri.
47. **Gyukatsu Motomura**: Crispy breaded beef cutlets cooked on your own personal tabletop mini-stone grill.
48. **Depachika Food Halls (Isetan / Takashimaya basement)**: Underground gourmet department store food paradises.
49. **Nishiki Market Snacks (Kyoto)**: "Kyoto's Kitchen" — quail eggs, matcha treats, and dango skewers.
50. **Matcha Parfait & Traditional Tea House (Uji / Kyoto)**: Rich green tea ice cream and traditional Japanese sweets.
51. **CoCo Ichibanya Curry**: Customizable Japanese curry with chicken katsu and adjustable spice levels.
52. **Yakitori Alley under Yurakucho Train Tracks**: Cold draft beer and charcoal chicken under rattling train lines.
53. **Sushiro / Kura Revolving Conveyor-Belt Sushi**: Automated sushi delivery with gachapon game rewards.
54. **Pork Loin Tonkatsu at Tonki / Maisen**: Deep-fried pork cutlets with shredded cabbage and sesame sauce.
55. **Japanese Whisky Tasting**: Yamazaki, Hakushu, and Hibiki flights at Tokyo/Kyoto highball bars.

### ⛩️ IV. Historic Shrines, Temples, Sumo & Ryokan Stay
56. **Overnight in a Traditional Ryokan with Private Onsen & Kaiseki Dinner**: Sleep on tatami mats, wear yukata robes, soak in hot springs, and enjoy a Kaiseki dinner & breakfast.
57. **Sumo Stable Morning Practice Visit (Asageiko, Ryogoku)**: Sit inches from the ring as active professional sumo wrestlers train during early morning practice in Tokyo.
58. **Fushimi Inari Taisha (Kyoto)**: Thousands of vivid vermilion torii gates winding up Mt. Inari.
59. **Kinkaku-ji / Golden Pavilion (Kyoto)**: Zen temple plated in real gold leaf overlooking a reflecting pond.
60. **Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa, Tokyo)**: Tokyo's oldest temple featuring the massive Kaminarimon lantern gate.
61. **Meiji Jingu Shrine (Shibuya, Tokyo)**: Peaceful Shinto shrine surrounded by a 170-acre forest in central Tokyo.
62. **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (Kyoto)**: Towering bamboo stalks rustling in the wind.
63. **Kiyomizu-dera Temple (Kyoto)**: Historic wooden stage built without nails offering panoramas over Kyoto.
64. **Nijo Castle Nightingale Security Floors (Kyoto)**: Shogun's palace with wooden floors engineered to squeak like birds for security.
65. **Himeji Castle ("White Heron", Hyogo)**: UNESCO World Heritage Site — Japan's best-preserved original castle.
66. **Gion Geisha District at Dusk (Kyoto)**: Wooden teahouses, lantern-lit alleys, and potential geiko sightings.
67. **Nara Park Deer & Todai-ji Temple**: 1,000+ free-roaming deer bowing for crackers around a giant bronze Buddha.
68. **Traditional Tea Ceremony Experience**: Learn the calm art of preparing matcha with a tea master.
69. **Imperial Palace East Gardens (Tokyo)**: Walk the stone foundations of the former Edo Castle.
70. **Zojo-ji Temple with Tokyo Tower Backdrop**: Striking juxtaposition of ancient temple and modern red tower.
71. **Sanjusangendo Temple (Kyoto)**: Hall filled with 1,001 life-sized carved statues of Kannon.
72. **Tennoji & Shitennoji Temple (Osaka)**: One of Japan's oldest official Buddhist temples.

### 🌸 V. Nature, Neighborhoods, Nightlife & Day Trips
73. **Mt. Fuji 5th Station / Lake Kawaguchiko Day Trip**: Volcanic rock fields, sweeping views of Mt. Fuji, and lake shore views.
74. **Shimokitazawa Vintage & Record Hunting**: Tokyo's indie neighborhood packed with retro thrifting and vinyl.
75. **Ueno Park & National Museum**: Sprawling park with lotus ponds, food stalls, and world-class museums.
76. **Philosopher’s Path Walk (Kyoto)**: Stone walkway alongside a canal lined with cherry trees and temples.
77. **Arashiyama Monkey Park Iwatayama (Kyoto)**: Hike up a mountain to feed wild Japanese macaques overlooking Kyoto.
78. **Harajuku & Takeshita Street**: Colorful pop culture, streetwear shops, and giant cotton candy.
79. **Shinjuku Private Room Karaoke**: Sing with English song catalogs and unlimited drink service.
80. **Japanese Baseball Game (Kyocera Dome / Meiji Jingu)**: High-energy fans, organized chants, and stadium food.
81. **Onsen Town Day Trip (Hakone / Kurama)**: Soak in natural mineral thermal hot springs surrounded by nature.
82. **Sagano Scenic Romantic Train (Kyoto)**: Open-air vintage train ride through Hozu River gorge.
83. **Kabukicho Neon Walk & Godzilla Head**: Walk Shinjuku's nightlife district under the giant hotel Godzilla.
84. **Roppongi Hills Mori Art Museum & Deck**: Contemporary art exhibit with skyline views of Tokyo Tower.
85. **Rikugien or Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden**: Serene Japanese landscape gardening with traditional tea houses.
86. **Tokyo Bay Evening Dinner Cruise**: View the illuminated Rainbow Bridge and Tokyo skyline from the water.
87. **Shinsaibashi Covered Shopping Arcade (Osaka)**: 600 meters of boutiques, shoe shops, and street snacks.
88. **Umeda Sky Building Floating Garden (Osaka)**: Twin towers linked 170m in the air by an open observatory.
89. **Yoyogi Park Street Performers & Buskers**: Weekend rockabilly dancers, musicians, and dog walks.
90. **Edo-Tokyo Open Air Architectural Museum**: Preserved historic Japanese storefronts, bathhouses, and homes.
91. **Yokohama Minato Mirai Waterfront Walk**: Red Brick Warehouses, Landmark Tower, and giant Ferris wheel.
92. **Osaka Castle Park & Moat**: Stroll around historic stone ramparts and surrounding gardens.

### 🛍️ VI. Shopping, Souvenirs & Unique Experiences
93. **Don Quijote (Mega Donki) Midnight Shopping**: 24/7 multi-story mega-discount store with Japanese snacks & gadgets.
94. **Hands (Tokyu Hands) Shibuya**: 8 floors of Japanese stationeries, tools, lifestyle goods, and travel gear.
95. **Kiddyland Harajuku**: Character goods paradise (Star Wars, Ghibli, Sanrio, Pokemon).
96. **Bic Camera / Yodobashi Camera Megastores**: Multi-floor electronics, cameras, watches, and tech toys.
97. **Nakasendo Trail Historic Walk Fragment**: Stroll preserved Edo post-town pathways.
98. **Japanese Knife Shopping (Kappabashi Kitchen Town, Tokyo)**: Hand-forged chef knives customized with engraving.
99. **Gachapon Kaikan (Akihabara)**: Hundreds of capsule toy vending machines for quirky Japanese souvenirs.
100. **Depachika Bento for the Shinkansen**: Pick up luxury bento boxes at Tokyo Station before boarding the Bullet Train.
101. **Animate Main Store (Ikebukuro)**: World's largest anime goods and manga department store.
102. **Sento Neighborhood Bathhouse Experience**: Join locals in a traditional public neighborhood bathhouse.
103. **Mandai / Book-Off Super Bazaar**: Pre-owned Japanese figures, watches, vintage leather, and clothing.
