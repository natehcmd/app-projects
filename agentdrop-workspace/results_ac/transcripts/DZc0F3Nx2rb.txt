I got this whole Jarvis setup running with a Mac Mini and Hermes agent.
The Mac Mini is the home base. This is where everything is hosted.
And Hermes is the agentic harness that lets Jarvis connect to tools,
remember context, and actually do work. Then I'm using separate Hermes profiles for all my
sub agents. Each one has its own instructions, skills, memory, and knowledge base,
so they're not all pulling from the same generic setup. All of them are connected to Slack,
telegram, eye message, and voice, so I can talk to my agents from pretty much anywhere.
And the part that makes it feel like Jarvis is the UI invoice. I built the UI with codecs,
and trained Jarvis's voice using 11 labs so he can respond back to me out loud.
