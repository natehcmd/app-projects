# AI Resume Optimizer Prompts

Based on the reel, here is the exact prompt chain to optimize your resume to pass ATS filters and stand out to hiring managers.

## Step 1: Analyze Resume against Job Description
Upload your resume and the job description to Claude or ChatGPT, and use this prompt:

> Act as a senior recruiter for this exact company and advise my resume against this job description. Give me a match score out of 100, the top 5 missing keywords, and the three red flags a hiring manager would spot in under 10 seconds.

## Step 2: Rewrite Experience Section
Stay in the same chat, and run this second prompt:

> Rewrite my experience section and naturally include those keywords and remove the red flags. Use the Google XYZ formula: accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z.

## Step 3: Optimize for Scanning
To ensure your resume actually gets read, run this final prompt:

> Now act as an ATS filter and a hiring manager reading 200 resumes at one setting. Scan my new resume and tell me which sections would get skipped and rewrite them so they actually stop the scroll.

## Bonus: Automate Applications (Advanced)
If using Claude Cowork or an agentic framework with web access:

> Now that my resume is optimized, connect to Indeed and apply to 10 jobs that have the highest chance of landing an interview for, and customize each application based on the job description.
